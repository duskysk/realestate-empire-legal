Privacy Policy - Real Estate Empire: Landlord

Last updated: 1 November 2026

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your game is saved on your iPhone. The only thing we receive is anonymous play statistics, and you can switch that off.

Your game

Your career (properties, money, goals, settings) is stored only on your iPhone. We never see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous play statistics

To learn how long people play and where the game gets boring, the app sends anonymous statistics to our own server (api.thelittlegreats.com, hosted in the EU). They contain:

  • a random ID created by the app on first launch (not your name, email, Apple ID, advertising ID or device ID; it changes if you reinstall),
  • game events such as starting a session, buying, selling or renovating a home, finishing a goal, and how long a session lasted,
  • the game month and your in-game money at that moment,
  • the app version, iOS version, iPhone model, language, region and time zone.

We do not store your IP address. The statistics are not linked to you, not used for advertising, not combined with other data and not shared with or sold to anyone. They are used only to improve the game.

To switch it off: open Settings (the gear icon) in the game and turn off Share anonymous play statistics. Nothing more is sent, and statistics not yet sent are deleted from your iPhone.

No third parties

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party code.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
