Property Trader Support

For bugs, questions or feedback, email michal.kralovic@gmail.com.

Please include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a description of what happened. Screenshots are optional; avoid including private information.

Quick answers

  • Buying and selling: tap a price bubble to buy, or tap the building for details and property actions.
  • Following several properties: star a property and open Watch to see pinned properties across neighborhoods.
  • Price history: property details show recorded quotes. A new career or recalibrated listing starts with a short history and collects more while you play.
  • Profit: the sale breakdown includes recorded purchase and improvement costs and the current sale fee. Rent is separate. Older saves may have a combined migrated cost basis.
  • Music: open the gear icon and switch Background music on or off.
  • Auto exits: unlock at Level 2 and execute at the next quote while the app is active; they can pass the target or stop price.
  • Offline saves: your career and preferences are saved on the device. Keep the app installed to retain its local data.

The game has no account, ads, purchases or real-money investing. Prices update while you play; there is no catch-up market simulation when you return.
