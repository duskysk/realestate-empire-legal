Privacy Policy

Property Trader: Real Estate
Updated 2 November 2026

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay analytics or advertising data to the developer.

Your game stays on your device

Your career, properties, in-game money, goals, watchlist and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. The app does not require an account or internet access. Deleting the app removes its local data; operating-system backups are controlled by your device settings.

No tracking or advertising

The app has no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers, in-app purchases or third-party network services. Its music is bundled with the app. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photo library.

Apple services

Apple operates the App Store and TestFlight and may process download, diagnostic or beta-feedback information under Apple's own policies. Those services are separate from the app's local gameplay.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, the developer receives the email address, message and any attachments you send and uses them to answer your request. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Support messages are not used for advertising.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the app's privacy practices change. Questions: michal.kralovic@gmail.com.
