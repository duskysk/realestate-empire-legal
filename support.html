Support - Real Estate Empire: Landlord

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to michal.kralovic@gmail.com. We read every message.

Quick answers

  • How do I play? Buy a home on the Deals tab, then tap Next month to collect rent. Tap Auto to let the months play by themselves.
  • Cash went below $0. Sell a property on the Portfolio tab; the game continues as soon as cash is positive.
  • The city asked me something during Auto. Auto-play picks the answer that changes nothing. Pause it to answer questions yourself.
  • Music or sounds. Switch them in Settings (the gear icon).
  • Start over. Settings > Restart career.
  • Is it really free? Yes. No ads, no energy, no timers and nothing to buy.
